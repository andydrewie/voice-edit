## no-ai-slop

Voice Edit is a Codex-focused adaptation informed by `petergyang/no-ai-slop`:

- Repository: <https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop>
- Reviewed baseline: `d30eddb9e04562234f2070b5ee63ca4649d9a05e`
- Author: Peter Yang
- License: MIT
- Retained license: [`LICENSES/no-ai-slop-MIT.txt`](LICENSES/no-ai-slop-MIT.txt)

The adaptation retains the underlying goal of minimum-effective, voice-preserving prose editing and general editorial pattern concepts. It adds an explicit-only Codex gate, a preservation-first priority order, untrusted-input and file-authority boundaries, audit/edit modes without authorship scoring, contextual rather than categorical pattern treatment, progressive disclosure, Agent Plugins packaging, and deterministic lexical-drift checks.
